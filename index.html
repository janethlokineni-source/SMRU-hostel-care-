Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will my complaint be resolved? ↓
Resolution times depend on the urgency level. Critical issues (e.g. electrical short circuits, water pipeline bursts) are attended within 4 hours. High urgency issues are targeted within 24 hours, and normal routine maintenance within 48 hours.
Can other students see my private complaint? ↓
No! Security and privacy are strictly enforced. Students can only see their own private complaints and authorized public announcements from the warden.
What should I do if a technician marks my complaint resolved but the issue is not fixed? ↓
You can easily reopen the complaint! Simply click on your complaint in your dashboard and click the "Reopen Complaint" button. This will alert the supervisor and record an escalation note.
How does the Dhobi laundry complaint process work? ↓
When you submit a Dhobi complaint with your laundry token/tag number and missing clothes description, it routes directly to the Dhobi Manager and hostel dhobi desk for immediate tracking.
